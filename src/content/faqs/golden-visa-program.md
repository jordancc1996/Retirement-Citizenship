A Golden Visa is a residency-by-investment program that allows individuals and their families to obtain legal residency in a country by making a qualifying investment. These programs typically offer a path to citizenship after a certain period and provide benefits such as visa-free travel, access to healthcare, and the ability to live, work, or study in the country.
