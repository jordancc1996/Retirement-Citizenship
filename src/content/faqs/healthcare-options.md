Many countries popular with American retirees offer excellent healthcare systems at a fraction of US costs. European countries like Portugal, Greece, and Italy have universal healthcare systems that residents can access. Private health insurance in these countries is also significantly more affordable than in the US, often costing $100-300 per month for comprehensive coverage.
