The cost of retiring abroad varies significantly depending on the country and lifestyle you choose. Many popular retirement destinations in Portugal, Greece, Italy, and Argentina offer a high quality of life at 30-50% less than living in the United States. Monthly expenses can range from $2,000 to $4,000 for a comfortable lifestyle, including housing, healthcare, food, and entertainment.
