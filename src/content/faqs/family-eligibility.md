The Golden Visa can include your spouse, dependent children who are full-time students, and even dependent parents. The key word here is dependent, and it's important to consult with your lawyer about what legally constitutes a dependent in this context. There's some misinformation floating around about who qualifies, so professional guidance is essential. The challenge with including children is that if they're working even part-time and not just full-time students, they may not qualify as dependents. You need professional advice for your specific situation. There is a process for including dependents, spouses, and full-time student children on the application, but you should confirm your particular circumstances with your lawyer before moving forward with any assumptions about who can be included.
