> The pursuit of freedom, luxury and profound cultural immersion finds its ultimate expression in the world of yachting. Yachting is a lifestyle that unlocks exclusive access to historic ports, secluded anchorages and a community of likeminded individuals all while providing the comfort and prestige of privacy on the water.

Three Mediterranean nations stand out as premier destinations for yachting hopefuls and each offers a unique flavor of the yachting experience. **Portugal** offers a strategic Atlantic gateway and high-end beaches, **Greece** offers a paradise of countless islands, and **Italy** offers coastal elegance and la dolce vita (the good life).

Many seasonal yachters leverage the power of the golden visa of each country to invest in their favorite destination and to create an even more comfortable environment for themselves. From the year-round sailing conditions of the Algarve to the ancient ruins of the Aegean and the glamour of Italy's Riveras, golden visa investors have been leveraging their travel documents to enjoy everything these countries offer and redefine the pinnacle of luxury travel.

## Yachting in Portugal

Portugal has 1,000 miles of Atlantic shoreline and over 300+ days of sunshine. The country also has direct access to both the Mediterranean and transatlantic routes. The yachting lifestyle in Portugal blends freedom and prestige to give yachters an invaluable experience. Americans have been flocking to Portugal for the last 5 years in droves and have indulged in the Portuguese yachting lifestyle. Yachting in Portugal is both aspirational and remarkably practical because the country offers a seamless entry into the European maritime community.

There are many high-end Portuguese marinas but three stand out as the premier blue-chip marinas in the country and have become the favorites of the international yachting community:

### Cascais Marina

Cascais Marina is just 30 minutes from Lisbon's international airport and serves as the nation's cosmopolitan gateway. It is a place where global yachting enthusiasts gather and are drawn to its famous regattas and sophisticated atmosphere. Its proximity to a major capital allows for a unique lifestyle which can be blended with days of sailing in the morning, golfing in the evening and nights enjoying the vibrant culture and dining in Lisbon.

### Vilamoura Marina

Vilamoura Marina is located in the Algarve region and is one of Portugal's crown jewels. This marina is the largest in Southern Europe and is the preferred destination for superyachts. The surrounding area is a master planned community of championship golf courses, private beach clubs and a thriving expat community.

### Lagos Marina

Lagos Marina is located further west and offers a different flavor of luxury. The atmosphere is enriched by 16th century forts and a charming old town. The focus is less on being seen and more on the intrinsic pleasure of sailing where yachters can explore the dramatic cliffs and secluded coves of the Algarve.

Portugal's unique combination of year-round sailing and strategic access to both the Atlantic and Mediterranean makes it an irresistible draw for Americans looking to embed the yachting lifestyle into their European experience.

## Yachting in Greece

Greece offers a destination that resonates with history and is a nation that has long been defined by its relationship with the sea. The yachting experience in Greece offers unparalleled diversity and scale because of the 2,000 Greek islands scattered across the Ionian and Aegean seas. These ancient waters provide a stunning backdrop of historic ruins, cinematic villages and some of the most beautiful anchorages in the world. The appeal for international visitors lies in this unique fusion of world-class sailing and vibrant local culture.

### Athens and Its Marinas

The journey for many yacht enthusiasts begins near Athens. The coastline around the capital is home to several of Europe's most significant marinas which offer state-of-the-art facilities and strategic starting points for any adventure.

One of the key Athenian hubs is **Marina Zeas** which is located in Piraeus and is one of the best superyacht marinas in the Mediterranean. The Marina Zeas has a rich history as a former naval base for ancient fleets and was modernized for the 2004 Olympics. Other key Athenian hubs include **Flisvos Marina** and **Alimos Marina**. These facilities provide secure mooring and a complete ecosystem of support services from maintenance and provisioning to fine dining and entertainment.

### Ionian and Aegean Odysseys

Greece's sailing areas are divided into two distinct seas. The **Ionian Sea** is located on Greece's western coast and the Ionian islands are known for their lush green landscapes, calmer waters and beautiful architecture. Islands like Corfu, Lefkada and Kefalonia offer more protected sailing conditions and make them ideal for families or those new to yachting. Marinas such as Gouvia Marina in Corfu and Marina Lefkada provide excellent bases for exploring and learning.

The **Aegean Sea** is home to the most iconic Greek island groups. The Cyclades are a magnet for the international jet set. The Dodecanese offer a rich tapestry of history with medieval castles and ancient ruins. The Sporades and Saronic islands provide further opportunities for exploration. The Aegean's famous Meltemi winds in the summer offer a chance for exhilarating sailing for more experienced crews.

Greece provides a complete spectrum of yachting experiences. It is a country where one can anchor in a secluded cove for a swim in the morning, explore a 2,500 year old temple in the afternoon and dine in a world class restaurant by nightfall. Americans thinking of making Greece their home for vacations have considered the golden visa for its lower minimum investment threshold—and applications have surged recently.

## Yachting in Italy

Italy's flair for la dolce vita extends to its magnificent coastlines. The country has over 7,500 kilometers of shore from the rugged cliffs of the Amalfi Coast to the chic beaches of Sardinia. Italy offers a yachting journey that is as diverse as it is glamorous. It is a destination that captivates with natural beauty, historical depth and an unwavering commitment to luxury.

### Sardinia and the Italian Riviera

Sardinia is an island nestled in the heart of the Mediterranean and it is widely regarded as a true yachting paradise. The Costa Smeralda is legendary for its pristine beaches, translucent waters and exclusive ambience. **Porto Cervo** is a marina that exudes Italian sophistication. Superyachts line a harbor surrounded by designer boutiques, upscale restaurants and luxury hotels which make it a favored playground for the global elite. **Marina di Portisco** serves as a tranquil gateway to the region and offers state-of-the-art facilities and convenient access to the wonders of the Sardinian coast.

On the mainland, the Italian Riviera is home to **Porto Mirabello** in La Spezia. This modern marina is specifically designed to accommodate superyachts and provides a base for exploring the enchanting fishing villages of the Cinque Terre and the iconic harbor of Portofino.

### The Amalfi Coast

The Amalfi Coast consists of a breathtaking landscape of dramatic cliffs, terraced vineyards and picturesque towns that cling to the mountainside. A yacht charter here is the ultimate way to experience the region and offers a perspective that is unavailable from land.

The best time to cruise this region is during the Mediterranean summer from May to September when the weather is warm and the sea is calm. The experience is incredible, from hiking the famous Path of the Gods trail to dining on fresh seafood at a family-run trattoria. The region is a testament to Italy's ability to seamlessly blend natural splendor with a sophisticated and deeply enjoyable lifestyle.
