> The decision to pursue a second citizenship or residency through investment is a choice that shouldn't be taken lightly because it is a major commitment of capital. We've analyzed a few successful applicants from the United States and Canada and have found the ideal client these services benefit by finding the common denominator in all of their circumstances.

The individuals are not always ultra-high net worth and aren't always focused primarily on capital or asset preservation. They are strategic, research oriented and ethically inclined. They approach the process not as a simple transaction but as a complex multi-faceted investment requiring rigorous due diligence and a clear understanding of their long-term goals. They are making this strategic investment so that they can enjoy the fruits of health and retirement as well as investing in the forefront of a new economy.

## Quality of Life, Security, and Health as Primary Drivers

The primary driver for this ideal client is often a desire for enhanced quality of life, security and health. Their motivation extends beyond mere financial diversification. They are seeking a stable, safe and welcoming culture. They are data driven and often cite global peace indices and other objective metrics to justify their investment. One applicant noted they always checked the global peace index to confirm a country's safety metrics. This decision is typically made in consultation with a partner or family member.

## The Prudent and Sophisticated Approach

The ideal client is prudent and sophisticated. They begin the process by conducting a thorough internal audit of their personal wealth. They distinguish between their core investments and non-core investments. This framework allows them to accurately define their risk tolerance for the investment component of the Golden Visa process.

It is important for this client to view the required investment as just that—investment—and not as a fee. They actively seek options with genuine return on capital potential over the mandatory holding period but understand that deep research of risks is paramount to the success of this process. They favor investments in growth-oriented vehicles such as venture capital or private equity funds over passive or fixed return options. They want their ideal fund to demonstrate authenticity and a clear benefit to the host country's economy. They also consider tax implications and seek programs that may offer benefits like the exclusion of global income from local taxes.

## Skepticism of Conflicts of Interest

The strategic investor is skeptical of potential conflicts of interest from marketers or salespeople of investment migration programs. They take ownership of the due diligence process and conduct thorough interviews with multiple brokers and advisors. They understand that some advisors may be incentivized by referral fees and that could compromise the objectivity of their recommendations. They apply a strict set of criteria and value ethics, responsiveness, depth of knowledge and quality of communication above all else. They seek to build quality and trustworthy relationships that will endure throughout the multi-year process.

## Proactive Engagement Throughout the Process

This client is proactive and engaged. They do not delegate the entire process blindly. They are willing to narrow down a large list of potential investment options through extensive research, phone interviews and even international travel to meet fund principals in person. This level of engagement is necessary to verify the fund managers' claims and ensure its operations are legitimate and aligned with the strategic investor's goals.

The strategic investor combines personal conviction with meticulous research to transform the complex second citizenship path into a successful and strategic endeavor that will grant life changing ROI.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How should I structure my investment due diligence?

A systematic approach is recommended:

1. **Financial Audit:** Distinguish between "Core" investments (needed for living) and non-core investments to establish a clear risk profile for the investment.
2. **Return Potential:** Since the investment must be held for five years or more it makes sense for strategic investors to seek options with real return potential. These options include venture capital or private equity funds but be sure to be cautious when reading about risky funds that offer a "guaranteed return" or "xyz% return".
3. **Authenticity:** Look for funds that demonstrate authenticity and a clear benefit for the host country. Avoid funds that appear to be quick packages solely for visa applicants.
4. **Tax Strategy:** Consider the host country's tax programs as part of the investment decision.

### What are the key benefits of obtaining an EU residency permit?

- **Schengen Zone Travel:** Visa free travel within the Schengen Zone
- **Plan B:** Possibility to move to the host country if that is desired
- **Permanent Residency and Path to Citizenship:** The opportunity to obtain permanent residency and citizenship afterwards
- **Healthcare Arbitrage:** Healthcare is significantly lower in EU countries versus the United States
- **Retirement Arbitrage:** Retirement in other countries can be substantially lower than retirement in the United States
