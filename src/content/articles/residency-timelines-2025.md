## 1. Portugal D7 Visa

The Portugal D7 Visa is designed for retirees and individuals with a reliable passive income - such as pensions, rental earnings, or dividends. Instead of requiring a significant upfront investment, this visa places priority on consistent financial stability.

### Minimum Financial Requirement

To qualify, applicants need to show a steady passive income of at least €870 per month for the primary applicant.

### Main Benefits

The D7 Visa offers freedom to travel throughout the Schengen Area without restrictions. After maintaining legal residency for five years, holders can apply for permanent residency in Portugal. With its mild climate, affordable cost of living, and welcoming atmosphere, Portugal stands out as an attractive choice for long-term residency.

## 2. UAE Golden Visa

The UAE Golden Visa is a residency program tailored for investors, entrepreneurs, and skilled professionals who want to secure long-term residency in the UAE. Positioned as a gateway to one of the world's most vibrant business hubs, this program provides a range of appealing benefits, making it an attractive option for those looking to live and work in the region.

## 3. Singapore GIP

The [Singapore Global Investor Programme](https://www.edb.gov.sg/en/incentives-and-programmes/global-investor-programme.html) (GIP) provides an opportunity for high-net-worth individuals to gain residency in one of Asia's leading financial hubs. As of now, the official 2025 requirements - including the necessary investment amount, processing timelines, and eligibility criteria - have not been released.

## 4. Greece Golden Visa

Greece's Golden Visa has emerged as a sought-after option for securing EU residency, particularly for those interested in real estate investment. Its relatively low cost compared to other European programs has made it a favorite among international investors.

### Minimum Investment (USD)

To qualify, you'll need to invest at least €250,000 (around $270,000) in real estate.

### Timeline to Initial Residency

The application process is relatively quick. You can expect to receive your initial residency within 2 to 3 months after submitting your application. Once approved, permanent residency is granted right away.

## 5. Malta MRVP

Malta's MRVP stands out as another appealing option for securing EU residency, offering unique benefits alongside Greece's streamlined process and attractive real estate opportunities.

### Main Benefits

The MRVP opens doors to greater mobility and business opportunities within the EU. It allows successful applicants - and their eligible family members - to live, work, and study in the EU, all while benefiting from Malta's appealing tax system and financial advantages.

## 6. Swiss Residency

Switzerland offers a pathway to residency through a temporary B Permit, which is renewable on an annual basis.

## 7. Montenegro Real Estate Residency

The Montenegro Citizenship by Investment program, which allowed individuals to gain residency and eventually citizenship through investment, officially ended on December 31, 2022.

## 8. Malaysia MM2H

The [Malaysia My Second Home](https://mm2h.com/) (MM2H) program offers long-term residency for those looking to settle in Malaysia. Recent updates to the program focus on ensuring applicants have solid financial backing and are actively involved in the local community.

### Main Benefits

The MM2H program comes with a range of perks. It offers a renewable long-term visa with multiple entries, which also extends to immediate family members. Other advantages include tax exemptions on foreign-sourced income, access to high-quality healthcare, opportunities to invest in real estate, and simplified rules for importing personal vehicles.

## 9. Cyprus Residency by Investment

Cyprus offers a streamlined investment program that provides a quick route to European residency. This initiative replaced the country's previous citizenship-by-investment program, which ended on November 1, 2020.

### Timeline to Initial Residency (Months)

The processing time is approximately 2 months from the date of application submission. Including the time needed for document preparation, the entire process typically takes 6–9 months.

## 10. United States EB-5 Visa

The United States [EB-5 Immigrant Investor Program](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/permanent-workers/eb-5-immigrant-investor-program) offers a straightforward path to U.S. permanent residency through investment.

### Main Benefits

The EB-5 program offers a direct route to U.S. permanent residency without requiring employer sponsorship or family ties. Portugal's D7 Visa and Greece's Golden Visa offer two distinct residency options, each catering to different lifestyles and priorities.
