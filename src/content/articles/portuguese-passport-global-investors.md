> The Portuguese passport has become one of the world's most powerful programs over the last decade. Portugal's passport is consistently ranked top five globally for travel freedom and grants visa free access to 190+ countries. The Portuguese passport also grants full EU citizenship rights and a stable gateway to Europe's business ecosystem.

## Portugal Leads the Innovative Investment Arena

### 1. Political Stability and Innovative Policy

Portugal has maintained a reputation as one of Europe's most politically stable jurisdictions which has made the country attractive for investment. Recent reforms have positioned it as an innovation-driven economy with thriving hubs in Lisbon, Porto and Braga which have startups in cutting edge industries like AI, fintech, energy, therapeutics and biotech. For global investors and family offices, Portuguese citizenship/residency is a vote for encouraging European innovation.

### 2. The Golden Visa Program

The Portuguese golden visa program allows investors to obtain residency by committing capital to regulated investment funds. The Portuguese Securities Market Commission (CMVM) regulates a majority of golden visa investment funds. These regulations ensure the funds are structured to meet Golden Visa requirements. This pathway aligns perfectly with investors who want to make a difference in the Portuguese economy. Unlike property-based routes, fund based golden visa investments actively support improvement within the Portuguese economy by betting on innovation, job creation and retaining quality talent within Portugal's borders.

### 3. Access to the Broader EU Market

The golden visa is a pathway to permanent residency and eventually citizenship by naturalization. Once naturalized, Portuguese citizens have the right to live, work, study, and invest across all 27 EU countries. This includes participation in EU startup programs, funding opportunities and cross-border syndications. An investor that holds a Portuguese passport can co-invest, launch funds or build startups anywhere in the EU under a unified regulatory umbrella. This creates a distinct comparative advantage for an investor by introducing new opportunities across many jurisdictions.

### 4. Global Talent Acquisition

Portugal's visa policies mixed with its quality of life (300+ days of sun is pretty nice) have made it a hub for founders from around the world interested in seeking entry into the European market.

## Portugal is a Portal to Europe's Future

The strength of the Portuguese passport is access. Access to funding, networks, healthcare, opportunities and a lifestyle that encourages innovation. Portugal's golden visa program is a bridge for forward thinking investors to a new and flourishing ecosystem. Holding a Portuguese passport is a strategic way to be a part of this transformation and benefit from it.

## Why Does the Portuguese Golden Visa Matter for Global Investors?

- **Global Mobility:** The investor has access to 27 EU economies
- **EU Investment Access:** The investor can invest across European markets and has access to EU networks
- **Innovation:** The investor has exposure to top tier startups across EU markets
- **Tax and Fund Benefits:** Investor friendly tax benefits

Portugal's golden visa has evolved from a travel document into a strategic asset for investors interested in being involved in innovation. For investors and entrepreneurs looking to participate in the EU's venture capital economy, the Portuguese golden visa is a must have and the most efficient point of entry for globally minded investors.
